Founder

Who is Ace Clark?

Born and raised in NYC, Ace faced depression, anxiety, and thoughts of suicide while navigating his identity — often without the support he needed. That gap is still faced by Black boys in schools today.

DBBAW is his answer: pairing scholars with Black male mentors who share real strategies for overcoming life's challenges — going beyond mentorship to build lasting confidence.

His work has brought him alongside Black Thought of The Roots, Lecrae, and BJ The Chicago Kid, as well as a collaboration with Idris Elba on the documentary Paid in Full: The Battle for Black Music.

Giving back is fundamental to my identity as a father, brother, and son committed to enhancing my community.
